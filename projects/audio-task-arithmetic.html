Task Arithmetic for Audio Foundation Models

Can we carry a foundation model’s robustness into its fine-tuned versions?

Audio learning

Supervisor: Mohammad Hossein Rohban

Robustness after fine-tuning

We studied whether the robustness of an audio foundation model can be transferred to a model fine-tuned for a particular audio task. Our starting hypothesis was that audio tasks share structure and may vary in fewer ways than the image tasks usually studied in this context. We wanted to understand whether those properties could help us preserve robustness during adaptation.

Using task arithmetic

Task arithmetic represents a task-specific change as the difference between a fine-tuned model’s parameters and those of the original foundation model. These updates can then be combined to modify another model. We explored whether this could carry robustness into fine-tuned audio models, and how properties of the audio data affect that transfer.
