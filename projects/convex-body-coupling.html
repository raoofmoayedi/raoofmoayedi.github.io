A New Coupling for Sampling from Compact Convex Bodies

A coupling-based approach to sampling, with the aim of a more elementary convergence proof

Sampling from convex bodies

Supervisor: Kasra Alishahi

A direct route to convergence

Inspired by László Lovász’s work on random walks in convex bodies, we began developing a new coupling for sampling from compact convex bodies. Alongside the construction, we are working on a convergence proof that uses a more elementary argument than the advanced tools used in classical analyses.

A coupling describes two random processes jointly so that their relationship can be studied. For sampling, controlling how the two processes approach one another can help establish how quickly the sampler converges. Our aim is to design a coupling for which this behavior can be analyzed directly, giving a self-contained convergence argument.

Reference

László Lovász and Miklós Simonovits. Random walks in a convex body and an improved volume algorithm. Random Structures & Algorithms, 4(4), 359–412, 1993.
