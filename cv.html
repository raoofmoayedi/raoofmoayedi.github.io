Research interests

I’m interested in fundamental questions about how machine learning systems learn, reason, and handle uncertainty. My interests span privacy, robustness, machine unlearning, robust statistics, uncertainty quantification, LLM reasoning, training dynamics, and scientific deep learning.

Research experience

EPFLAugust 2026 – present

Research Intern · E3 Program · Prof. Olga Fink

Working at the intersection of graph neural networks and conventional numerical methods for fluid simulation. I am developing models that use the structure of numerical solvers to predict fluid evolution over larger time steps than those solvers can reliably take on their own. The aim is to maintain accurate long-term predictions while reducing the number of simulation steps and the overall computational cost.

University of CambridgeJune 2025 – March 2026

Remote Research Assistant · Dr. Amir Reza Asadi

Developed a geometry-adaptive algorithm for differentially private synthetic data. It uses the data’s intrinsic structure to improve accuracy and reduce computation, with stronger error guarantees than methods governed by the ambient dimension.

Geometry-Adaptive Mechanisms for Private Synthetic Data

Nanyang Technological UniversityJanuary – June 2025

Funded Visiting Research Student · Prof. Yew-Soon Ong

Worked on robust prompt tuning, superalignment, and task arithmetic, with a focus on adversarial robustness. Across these projects, I studied how adapting prompts, aligning models, and combining task-specific updates affect a model’s response to adversarial perturbations. The goal was to understand when these changes preserve robustness and when they introduce new vulnerabilities.

Imperial College LondonJune 2023 – June 2024

Remote Research Assistant · Dr. Seyed-Mohsen Moosavi-Dezfooli

Worked on transferring adversarial robustness from teacher models to smaller students through robust knowledge distillation. This work was published in IEEE TPAMI.

Allies Teach Better Than Enemies: Inverse Adversaries for Robust Knowledge Distillation

Also worked on two projects in adversarial robustness:

  • Robust data pruning: developed a method for selecting training examples and studied when pruning improves a classifier’s robustness.
  • Input-space geometry: studied the geometry of learned models under different architectural biases and how these differences relate to adversarial robustness.
