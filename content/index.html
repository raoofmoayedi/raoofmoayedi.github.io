EPFL

Research Intern · E3 Program

August 2026 – present

I’m working on graph neural networks that use the structure of conventional numerical solvers to predict fluid motion. The goal is to take larger time steps than the solvers can reliably take on their own, while keeping predictions accurate over long periods. Taking fewer steps would make these simulations less expensive.

With Prof. Olga Fink
