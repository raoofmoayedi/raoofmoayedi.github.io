Input-Space Geometry of CNNs and Transformers

How does a model’s architecture shape the regions it learns in input space?

Imperial College London

Research with Dr. Seyed-Mohsen Moosavi-Dezfooli

Connectivity and architecture

We compared CNNs and Transformers to study how their architectural biases affect the geometry of learned models in input space. We focused on the regions these models form: whether each region is connected or splits into separate components, and how these and other topological properties vary with the architecture.
