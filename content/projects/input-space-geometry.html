Input-Space Geometry of CNNs and Transformers

How does a model’s architecture shape the regions it learns in input space?

Imperial College London

Research with Dr. Seyed-Mohsen Moosavi-Dezfooli

Architecture and the shape of learned regions

We studied how different architectures and their inductive biases shape the geometry of learned models in input space. Comparing CNNs and Transformers, we focused on the connectivity of the regions they form and on their topological properties.

What we investigated

Inductive biases
How the assumptions built into an architecture influence the structure of the regions a model learns.
Connectivity
Whether a region forms a connected set or breaks into separate components, and how this differs across architectures.
Topology
How the broader structure of these regions varies between models, beyond their local shape.
