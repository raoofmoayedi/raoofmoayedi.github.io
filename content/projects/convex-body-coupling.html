A New Coupling for Sampling from Compact Convex Bodies

A coupling-based approach to sampling, with the aim of a more elementary convergence proof

Sampling from convex bodies

Supervisor: Kasra Alishahi

A direct route to convergence

Inspired by László Lovász’s foundational work on random walks and sampling in convex bodies, we began developing a new coupling for sampling from compact convex bodies. Our goal is to establish convergence through a direct coupling argument, with less reliance on the advanced machinery used in classical analyses.

The project centers on constructing the coupling and proving that it leads to convergence. We are working toward a more elementary, self-contained proof that makes the role of the coupling explicit.

Foundational reference

A foundational treatment of random walks, sampling, and convergence in convex bodies is:

László Lovász and Miklós Simonovits. Random walks in a convex body and an improved volume algorithm. Random Structures & Algorithms, 4(4), 359–412, 1993.
