Task arithmetic and adversarial robustness

Transferring robustness through adaptation

We explored task arithmetic as a way to transfer robustness from audio foundation models to models fine-tuned for specific tasks. Our starting point was that audio and image data differ in their diversity and structure. We wanted to understand how properties specific to audio affect robustness and whether they can help guide its transfer during fine-tuning.

What we investigated

Properties of audio
Which characteristics of audio matter for robustness, and how they influence what can be transferred between models.
Fine-tuning
How adapting a foundation model to a specific audio task affects the robustness it already has.
Task arithmetic
Whether combining task-specific model updates can help carry robustness into fine-tuned models.
