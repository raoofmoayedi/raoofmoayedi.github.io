Robust Data Pruning

How do the choices we make before, during, and after data selection affect a model’s robustness?

Imperial College London

Research with Dr. Seyed-Mohsen Moosavi-Dezfooli

Three stages of pruning

We studied whether pruning training data can improve adversarial robustness. We examined each stage separately to understand how its choices affect the final model.

  1. 01

    Prepare the data and compute statistics

    We examined how data preparation changes the statistics used to assess training examples.

  2. 02

    Score examples and select a subset

    We studied the scoring rules and selection choices that determine which examples to keep.

  3. 03

    Train on the pruned data

    We examined how the training procedure affects robustness once the subset has been selected.
