CuPINN: training with residual curvature

A curvature penalty for training physics-informed neural networks.

Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering · 2025

With Mostafa Abbaszadeh and Mehdi Dehghan

Controlling the residual’s curvature

Physics-informed neural networks learn solutions to differential equations by penalizing violations of those equations. In this work, we also penalize the curvature of that residual across the input domain. CuPINN and its variants encourage a flatter residual surface. We also combine this approach with gradient-enhanced PINNs.

What CuPINN adds to training

Equation residual
Penalize violations of the differential equation.
Residual curvature
Also discourage sharp changes in the residual across the input domain.
Computation
Use trace estimation and finite differences without constructing the full Hessian.

The curvature is measured with respect to the inputs, not the model parameters.

Results

Across seven PDE benchmarks, including linear and nonlinear problems, the methods improve solution accuracy and residual error over the tested PINN and GPINN baselines. We also see improvements with relatively few training points.

Current work at EPFL

As an E3 research intern with Prof. Olga Fink, I am working on graph neural networks to predict how fluid systems behave over long periods. We aim to use physical knowledge to make these predictions less expensive than traditional simulations.
