Raoofmoayedi2000@gmail.com

I am interested in fundamental questions in machine learning, especially privacy, robustness, and machine unlearning.

I want to understand the limits these requirements place on learning and design algorithms with provable guarantees. My work so far has focused on differential privacy and robust learning, including the role of data geometry and training dynamics.

I am a master's student in computer science at Sharif University of Technology, with a background in mathematics and computer science.
